Chainer

A dark-fantasy tower defense for Android. Every tower is a chain: you draw it link by link from an anchor, and it swings through the enemies walking the road.

Chainer is made by one developer, András Benedek, in Hungary. It is coming to Google Play.

Contact

Questions, bug reports and privacy requests: the.chainer.game@gmail.com. You can also send a report from inside the game with the send feedback button in the pause menu or in settings.
