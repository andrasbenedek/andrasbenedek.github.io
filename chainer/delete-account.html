Delete your Chainer account

Chainer, a game by András Benedek · Last updated 16 November 2026

Chainer has an account only if you signed in to cloud save with SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE. Deleting it removes your Chainer account and the cloud copy of your progress. It never touches your Google account.

In the game

  1. Open SETTINGS and find CLOUD SAVE. If it reads not signed in, tap SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE and pick the account first; this syncs your progress once. Or write to me instead, below.
  2. If you play on other phones with the same Google account, tap SIGN OUT on each of them first. A phone that is still signed in can upload a new copy for up to an hour.
  3. Tap DELETE CLOUD SAVE, then DELETE. Google may ask you to pick your account once more to confirm it is you.

Settings then reads not signed in. Your progress on the phone stays, so you can keep playing. If a phone you did not sign out uploaded a copy afterwards, write to me and I will delete it.

Without the game

Email the.chainer.game@gmail.com from the address of the Google account you signed in with, and ask for your Chainer account to be deleted. I delete it and reply within one month, usually within a few days. If you cannot write from that address, say which address it was, and I will ask you to confirm from it before deleting anything.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
The cloud copy of your progressdeleted from the database at once; I keep no backups of it
Your Chainer account: Google account identifier, email, name and picture linkdeleted at once, then removed from Google's systems and backups within 180 days
Sign-in IP addresses and user agents logged by Firebasekept a few weeks for abuse prevention, then deleted
Progress on your phonekept; uninstall the game or clear its data to delete it
Android backup in your Google Drivekept; delete it in the Google Drive app under Backups

Crash reports and gameplay statistics are not linked to your account. A feedback report sent while you were signed in carries your account's identifier in its save files, and is kept 30 days. To have any of them deleted sooner, say so in the same email; the privacy policy explains what they hold and how long they are kept.
