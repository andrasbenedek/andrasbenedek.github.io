Chainer privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

In short:

  • The game works offline and needs no account. Your progress is saved on your phone, and Android may back it up to your Google account unless you turn backup off.
  • Cloud save is optional. If you sign in with Google, your account's email and identifier and a copy of your progress are stored with Google's Firebase until you delete them, which you can do in settings or on the account deletion page.
  • Crash reports and gameplay statistics are sent only if you tap SHARE when the game asks. You can turn this off at any time in settings.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, Google asks for your consent first. Owners of the ad-free pack get no ads, and the ad software never starts for them.
  • No data is sold.

Who is responsible

Chainer is made by András Benedek, an individual developer in Hungary, who is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: the.chainer.game@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, your settings and whether you own the ad-free pack are stored in the game's own storage on your phone. They are not sent anywhere except in the cases described below: Android's backup, unless you turn it off; cloud save, if you sign in; and a feedback report, if you send one. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes them from the phone. A backup Android has made stays in your Google account until you delete it.

Crash reports and gameplay statistics

On first launch the game asks whether you want to share diagnostics. Nothing is sent before you tap SHARE. If you tap DON'T SHARE, or turn share diagnostics off later in settings, both services below stop.

Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time with the settings toggle. Withdrawing does not affect what was sent before.

Sentry: crash reports and feedback

Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) receives:

  • Crash reports. Each one holds the error and where in the code it happened, your phone model, the Android version, the game's version, and the last few game events before the crash (for example, that a run started).
  • Feedback you send with the SEND FEEDBACK button. A report holds:
    • the text you write, and a name or email address only if you type one in;
    • the game's version and your platform;
    • the map, wave and lives if you send it during a run;
    • a replay of the run you are playing, or of the last run since you opened the game: the moves you made in it (the links you placed, the routes you drew, the waves you started) and when, so the developer can play the run again. It holds nothing about you or your phone;
    • your save files (progress, settings, the ad-free pack status, the cloud-save sync records, which hold your cloud-save account's identifier, and the copies cloud-save conflicts replaced);
    • the game's last 20 errors.

The game does not send your name, email or other identifying details to Sentry unless you type them into feedback. The one exception is cloud save: a feedback report sent from a phone that is signed in carries that cloud-save account's identifier in its save files, which I could match to the account's email. Data is stored in the EU (Germany) and kept for 30 days.

If diagnostics are off, the button reads copy a feedback report and copies the report to your clipboard instead. You decide where to paste it, for example into an email to the address above.

PostHog: gameplay statistics

PostHog (PostHog, Inc.) receives:

  • Gameplay events. For example: a run started, finished or was abandoned, and its map, waves, lives and duration; a chain built; a talent, boost or chain finish bought with in-game currency; a tutorial step reached or skipped; an ad shown; the ad-free pack viewed or bought.
  • App opened and app moved to the background.
  • Device and installation details. A random identifier the analytics software creates for this installation, your phone model, the Android version, the game's version and your language. PostHog also uses your IP address to estimate your country and city.

The identifier is not linked to your name, your Google account or your advertising ID. Data is stored in the EU and kept for up to one year. I use it to find out which parts of the game are too hard, which are confusing, and what to fix next.

Advertising

The free version uses Google AdMob, run by Google Ireland Limited in the EEA and the UK and by Google LLC elsewhere. It shows rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for an in-game reward, and an ad at the end of some runs.

Consent: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears before any ad is requested. You can accept personalised ads, refuse them, or choose purposes one by one. To change your answer later, tap personalised ads · CHANGE in settings. If you refuse personalised ads, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which are chosen by context, such as the app and your rough location. Outside those regions, ads may be personalised as local law permits.

What Google receives: your device's advertising ID, your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses it to show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same one, and prevent fraud. Its use is governed by Google's privacy policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, under Privacy → Ads or Google → Ads, depending on your phone.

Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), collected through Google's form.

Owners of the ad-free pack see no ads, and the game never starts the ad software for them.

Buying the ad-free pack

Payment is handled by Google Play, and I never see your card or payment details.

To check the purchase and let you restore it on another phone, the game uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt from Google Play, the product and purchase time, a random identifier it creates for this installation, device and app information, and your IP address. It keeps this for as long as the purchase needs to keep working, and deletes it on request.

Legal basis: performing the contract for the pack (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Cloud save

Cloud save copies your progress to the cloud so it can move to another phone or survive a reinstall. Nothing is uploaded, and no account exists, unless you tap SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE under CLOUD SAVE in settings and pick a Google account. It uses Firebase, a Google service (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA, Google LLC elsewhere), acting for me as processor:

  • Firebase Authentication creates a Chainer account linked to your Google account. It holds your Google account's identifier, your email address, and the name and profile picture link your Google account gives, with the times the account was created and last signed in. Settings shows the email so you know which account is signed in. To prevent abuse, Firebase also logs your IP address and your phone's user-agent string when you sign in, and keeps them for a few weeks. Firebase Authentication runs only in Google's data centres in the United States.
  • Cloud Firestore stores one copy of your progress, the same save the game keeps on your phone (no settings, no purchase status), under your account's identifier. Like every save file, it names the random identifier the game gave the phone that saved it last. It is stored in the EU (Google's eur3 region, Belgium and the Netherlands).

The game syncs when it starts, after each run, when you take the daily satchel, when you sign in and when you leave it: it uploads your progress, or downloads the cloud copy when another phone has saved more. If two phones both played before syncing, the one with more progress is kept, and the game keeps a copy of the other on this phone, one for each time it happens, which is never uploaded.

Signing out: SIGN OUT stops syncing on this phone. Your progress on the phone and the cloud copy both stay, and signing in again picks the copy up.

Deleting: DELETE CLOUD SAVE in settings, then DELETE, deletes the cloud copy and your Chainer account. Progress on the phone stays. Sign out on your other phones first: a phone that is still signed in can upload a new copy for up to an hour, and if one does, write to me and I will delete it. If you no longer have the game, the account deletion page explains how to ask me to delete them. Deleting your Chainer account does not touch your Google account.

Legal basis: providing the feature you asked for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Android backup

Android's own backup service (Auto Backup) copies the game's saved files to the Google Drive storage of the Google account on your phone: your progress and a spare copy of it, your settings, whether you own the ad-free pack, the cloud-save sync records, which hold your cloud-save account's identifier, and the copies cloud-save conflicts replaced. It may also copy the small settings files that the ad, purchase and statistics software keep on the phone, such as your answer to Google's consent form. It never copies the file holding the random identifier the game gives this installation, so a phone restored from a backup gets a new one; the progress file names the identifier of the phone that saved it last.

Android makes the backup about once a day, while the phone is idle, charging and on Wi-Fi. When you reinstall the game, or set up a new phone with the same Google account, Android puts the files back, so your progress is not lost. Google stores the backup encrypted, under its privacy policy. I never receive it and cannot see it.

To turn backup off, open your phone's Settings → Google → Backup (on some phones, Settings → System → Backup) and switch it off. This stops the backup for every app on the phone, not only Chainer, and a reinstall then starts the game from the beginning. To delete a backup that already exists, open the Google Drive app, go to Backups and delete this phone's backup.

Google Play

Downloading and updating the game through Google Play is covered by Google's own privacy policy. Google Play gives me statistics, such as install counts, and crash reports that do not identify you.

Transfers outside the EU

Sentry and PostHog store the game's data in the EU, and so does cloud save's copy of your progress. RevenueCat and Google may process data in the United States, and the cloud-save account is always held there, since Firebase Authentication runs only in the US. Where data leaves the European Economic Area, it is protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, whichever the provider uses.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can:

  • ask for a copy of your data;
  • have your data corrected or deleted;
  • restrict how it is used, or object to that use;
  • receive your data in a portable form;
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

Write to the.chainer.game@gmail.com. If you used cloud save, write from the email address of the Google account you signed in with, so I can find your account; for anything else, tell me roughly when you played or sent feedback, and I will find and delete what I can. I will reply within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Hungary, the Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH), or the authority where you live.

Children

Chainer is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect their data. Some countries set a higher age for consenting to data use online: 16 in Hungary and several other EU countries. If you are under that age, tap DON'T SHARE and refuse personalised ads unless a parent agrees. If you believe a child has sent me personal data, for example in feedback, write to me and I will delete it.

Security

Everything the game sends travels encrypted over HTTPS. I do not sell personal data, and I do not use it for automated decisions that affect you.
